AirDraw: hand gesture drawing app

A browser-based drawing app where you draw in the air using hand gestures, with no mouse or touch screen needed.

  • Real-time hand tracking with Google MediaPipe Hands.
  • Drawing on HTML5 Canvas with brush, colour and eraser controls.
  • Published online with GitHub Pages.
